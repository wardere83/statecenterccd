SCCCD's PM Dashboard Tutorial Video
Created by Bulle Consulting

Welcome to State Center's project management dashboard.
The information here was collected from the proposal you submitted with
your E E O I B P Grant Initiative application.
If anything is incorrect, please let us know as
soon as possible so we can update the platform.
The Overview gives you a quick picture of your program and grant.
Project Profile brings together the purpose and approach of your Faculty in Training program.
It's a helpful starting point for understanding the project and keeping your team aligned.
Goals and Objectives summarizes what your project is working toward.
Use this tab as a shared reference when discussing progress, planning activities, or preparing your next update.
Budget Breakdown gives you a high level view of how
grant funding is allocated across the two project years.
It helps your program and fiscal teams stay on the
same page as they review spending and plan ahead.
Timeline brings the implementation period and planned work into one view.
Contacts lists the current people supporting your project.
If anyone joins, leaves, or changes roles, please let us know immediately
so we can make the necessary updates and keep communication running smoothly.
Booking S M E gives you a place to arrange time with your subject matter expert.
Use it when you'd like support with a question, a challenge, or your next grant deliverable.
Spend Down Monitor brings budget balances, spending pace, and status indicators together.
Use it to support conversations with your fiscal team and identify items that may need follow up.
Forms brings your grant reporting and modification requests into one place.
Come here when you're ready to share a progress update or request a
change, and reach out to your grant monitor if you need guidance.
The Accessibility and Translate options help you adjust the dashboard to your reading and language preferences.
Choose the settings that make the information easier for you to use.
That's your dashboard at a glance.
We're here to help keep it useful, accurate, and current for your team.
You can return to the tutorial tab anytime to watch this video again or download a copy.
Thank you for all the work you're doing.
